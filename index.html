HITS ALGORITHM
Hyperlink-Induced Topic Search · Jon Kleinberg, 1999
1

THE IDEA

HITS models the web as a directed graph. Each page (node) has two scores: Hub (how well it links to good pages) and Authority (how many good hubs link to it). Good hubs point to good authorities, and good authorities are pointed to by good hubs — a mutual reinforcement.

2

INITIALIZATION

All Hub and Authority scores start at 1.0:

hub(v) = auth(v) = 1.0 for all v
3

AUTHORITY UPDATE

A page's authority = sum of hub scores of all pages that link TO it:

auth(v) = Σ hub(u)   for all u → v

Pages linked by many strong hubs get high authority.

4

HUB UPDATE

A page's hub score = sum of authority scores of all pages it links TO:

hub(u) = Σ auth(v)   for all u → v

Pages that link to many strong authorities get high hub scores.

5

NORMALIZATION

After each update, normalize both vectors by their L2 norm to prevent scores from growing unboundedly:

auth = auth / ||auth||₂    hub = hub / ||hub||₂
6

CONVERGENCE

Steps 3–5 repeat until the maximum change between iterations falls below threshold ε (typically 10⁻⁶). The algorithm converges to the principal eigenvectors of AᵀA (authorities) and AAᵀ (hubs).
